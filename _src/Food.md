For food in Italy, I've listed some popular foods that you should have when you're there. Similar to things to do, different places in Italy are known for the types of food.

## Pasta

You can find lots of pasta in Italy, anywhere you go it will be there. My favourite is fettuccine alfredo, I cant wait to eat that when I'm there. 

Based on what section of Italy you're in, the pasta options or recipies differ. For example, the south is closer to the coastline so there is more seafood involved in the food, and tomato sause is very popular there because they have a longer growing season.

## Pastries

Pastries are huge in Italian culture, there are so many different kinds and I'll list some:

- Amaretti cookies
- Canoli
- Tiramisu
- Pizzelle cookies
- Biscotti
- Croissants (specifically with Nutella)
- Tarallucci cookies

## Coffee

You will also find coffee shops everywhere with coffee, cappuccino, and espresso and more. 

Cappuccino is really popular, it's espresso with steamed and foamed milk. There is also some etiquette in coffee shops and drinking cappuccino is more common in the mornings.

## Pizza

The place to go for pizza is Naples, it's in the south where they get really good tomato growing seasons. 

These are some popular types of pizza:

- Margherita 
- Napoletana
- Nutella Pizza (more of a dessert)
- Capricciosa
